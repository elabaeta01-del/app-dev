# My Favorite Movie: White Chicks 🎬

White Chicks is a hilarious 2004 comedy film directed by Keenen Ivory Wayans.

---

## Plot Summary (in brief)

Two disgraced FBI agents, Kevin and Marcus Copeland (played by Shawn and Marlon Wayans), must go undercover as two rich white socialites to protect them from a kidnapping plot.

### Why I Like It

* **Iconic Characters:** The transformation and makeup are legendary.
* ** quotable lines:** It has many memorable and funny lines.
* **Great Pacing:** The comedy rarely slows down.

> "I'm going to go shopping, and then I'm going to get my nails done, and then I'm going to go shopping, and then I'm going to get my nails done." - Tiffany Wilson

**Fun Fact:** The film earned over $113 million worldwide!
